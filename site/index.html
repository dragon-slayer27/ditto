🦥 Slow and steady form filling

Type it once.
Ditto fills every form after that.

A Chrome extension that fills any web form from your private profile, using Gemma 4 running on your own computer through Ollama. Job portals, bank KYC, clinic intake, rental applications, college forms. Your passport number never leaves your laptop.

Install Ditto (free) Try the demo forms

Open source (MIT) · open-weight model (Apache 2.0) · no account, no cloud, no subscription

Browser autofill gives up

It knows your name and email. It has no idea what "Father's / Spouse's Name", "Aadhaar No. (last 4 digits only)" or "Name as per PAN, in BLOCK LETTERS" means.

Cloud AI fillers want your ID

To fill a KYC form with ChatGPT you'd paste your PAN, passport and address into someone else's server. Every time.

Ditto reads the form like you do

A small open model reads every label in context, so a "Name" under Nominee details gets your mother's name, not yours.

How it works

  1. Teach it once, lazily. Paste your resume or an old form into Ditto's profile page and Gemma pulls out the facts. Or just fill one form by hand and click Remember what I typed.
  2. Press Alt+Shift+D on any form. Gemma reads the labels and sections, and fields fill one by one, at sloth speed, while you sip your chai.
  3. Glance at the colours, then submit it yourself. Green = from your profile. Amber = please check. Grey = needs you.
  4. It gets lazier over time. Every answer you type teaches it, and a form it has seen before fills in under a second.

Honest numbers

I built five realistic forms (a React job application, an old table-layout bank KYC form, a clinic intake, a placeholder-only rental form and a dark-mode hackathon sign-up) with traps: nominee sections, BLOCK LETTERS, "last 4 digits only", consent boxes, essay questions and fields the profile simply can't answer. What Ditto will never do

  • Submit a form. You always press the button.
  • Tick "I agree" or "I declare". Consent is yours to give.
  • Touch passwords, card numbers, OTPs or captchas.
  • Invent things. A tripwire checks every value against your profile; anything it can't trace back turns amber.
  • Send your data anywhere. It only talks to Ollama on 127.0.0.1, and can't even read a page until you press the button.

Install (5 minutes)

  1. Install Ollama, then run ollama pull gemma4:e4b (about 6 GB; use gemma4:e2b on 8 GB machines).
  2. Download ditto-extension.zip and unzip it (or clone the repo).
  3. Open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  4. On the profile page that opens, paste your resume or load the demo person, Meera.
  5. Open any form (or the demo forms) and press Alt+Shift+D.

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers. No Ollama settings to change: Ditto handles the connection itself.

Why an open model?

Forms are where we hand over the most sensitive facts we have: ID numbers, addresses, health conditions, family members. An open-weight model running locally is the only way I'd trust a robot with them. It works offline, costs nothing per form, can't be switched off or repriced, and anyone can read every line of the code that touches their data.
